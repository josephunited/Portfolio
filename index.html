✦ About Me

Harmonizing Tech & Acoustics

Bridging logical engineering and musical expression

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student with a deep passion for software development, artificial intelligence, and sound engineering. I thrive on learning new tools, frameworks, and paradigms that push the boundaries of what's possible, especially in creative tech.

Beyond code, I'm an accomplished musician. I am a keyboardist in the popular Malayalam progressive rock band YUVA, actively performing on live stages and contributing to collaborative audio and tech initiatives. I have competed in 10+ major inter-school and inter-collegiate music competitions, securing several first-prize finishes.

My musical discipline is backed by formal training, having successfully completed Grade 8 in Electronic Keyboard from Trinity College London. I also engage in music production, synthesizing beats, and composing digital audio works.
